Did you know?
There are tons of trashes produced each year

The world produces around 350 million tonnes of plastic waste each year. A recent high-quality studies suggest between 1 or 2 million tonnes of plastic enters the ocean annually.
So, how do we reduce this?

3R's

3R (Reduce, Reuse & Recycle) is the simplest way you can do to minimize our trash production rate!

Reduce

Reduce itself means to make something smaller or less in amount, degree or size!

    How?

  • Buy stuffs you only need. Make sure you don't overconsume something.
  • Try to compost food waste & other organic materials.
  • If you have something that you don't use, donate them to people in need so they can use it again.

Reuse

According to Oxford Language, Reuse is an action on using something more than once!

    How?

  • When going shopping, use a reusable shopping bags made from cloth. Avoid using paper bags as it can only be used once.
  • Convert old clothes or towels you don't use into a cleaning rag.
  • Try to bring your own tumblers or water bottle instead of using plastic bottle.
  • Do not throw away unused papers, you can still reuse them to wrap gifts or use the empty space to write something.

Recycle

Recycling is the process of collecting and processing materials that would be thrown away as trashes into a new product!

    What Are Materials That Can Be Recycled?

  • Glass
  • Paper
  • Plastics
  • Dry organic waste
  • Aluminium
  • You can give those trashes to an organization for them to recycle into a useful products!
