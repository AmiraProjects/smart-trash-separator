About This Website

What is the Goal of this Web?

This web aims to raise people's awareness about Earth's condition these days. This web also promote 3R (Reduce, Reuse and Recycle) to the society so many more people care about the Earth. I feel that we - as humans - are responsible of our planet as our activities has harm the Earth since years ago.

Most importantly, I wanted to contribute into realizing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG). Specifically, SDG Number and 15 (Life on Land). SDG 15 itselfs aims to Promote Sustainable Use Of Terrestrial Ecosystem, Sustainably Manage Forest, Combat Desertification, Halt And Reverse Land Degradation And Halt Biodiversity Loss. One of the way to do that is to apply 3R - which is what I'm trying to promote!

Hopes from This Web

I hope - through this web - more people are inspired to apply the 3R in their daily lives started from simplest act. In the future, I also hope there will be a smart trash separator in real life like in my web. Maybe the trash separator would be in a form of a sensor at trash bin. When someone threw the wrong trashes into the wrong bin, the bin's lid won't open - forcing the person to throw their trashes into the right bin. I know, we already had that kind of technology but it hasn't been spread widely throughout the world. So, I hope that technology could be spread widely in Indonesia.
